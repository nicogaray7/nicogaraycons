Cookie policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. This page explains which cookies and trackers nicogaray.com uses, what they are for, and how to set your preference.

Audience measurement

The site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to know how many people visit it, which pages and case studies are read, and which buttons are used (for example "Book a call"). These statistics are used only to improve the site. No data is used for advertising purposes.

Google may process some data outside the European Union, notably in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Advertising

Some pages load the Google AdSense script. Ad storage is denied by default via Google's consent mode: no advertising or ad-personalization cookie is set.

Other third-party services

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address. No cookie is set.
  • Google Tag Manager: a tag-management tool, it does not itself set a cookie.
  • Cal.com, Malt, LinkedIn, partners: these services are only contacted if you click their link, and then apply their own rules. Referral links go through a redirect page on the site that sets no cookie.

Your choice

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline audience measurement. You can change your mind at any time with the button below, or with the 🍪 button at the bottom of the screen. Declining does not prevent you from using the site.

You can also delete cookies from your browser settings.
